# Short description

Standups, kudos, team rewards, and fair picks—all in Slack.

# Long description

Small rituals. Stronger teams.

Kick brings everyday team rituals into Slack. Keep everyone aligned, recognize great work, and share responsibilities with simple commands—without adding another dashboard to your day.

- Standups with /sync: Share progress, plans, and blockers so your team knows what needs attention.
- Recognition with /kudos: Thank teammates and celebrate their contributions.
- Team rewards with /coins: Send virtual coins as a token of appreciation.
- Fair picks with /pick: Randomly select teammates for tasks and team rituals.

From daily check-ins to well-deserved shout-outs, Kick helps make teamwork more visible and appreciation more frequent.

Learn more at https://kick.bozmoz.com.

Kick is an independent third-party integration for Slack.
